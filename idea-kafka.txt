Some extra things which I can think about
1. Claim extraction fails in case of Interlinked sentences like this
Claim: "The director of the film that won the 2020 Oscar for Best Picture was born in Seoul."
    You cannot verify this with one passage. You need to:
        Hop 1: Which film won Best Picture 2020? → Parasite.
        Hop 2: Who directed Parasite? → Bong Joon-ho.
        Hop 3: Where was Bong Joon-ho born? → Daegu, South Korea.
        Verify: The claim says Seoul. Refuted.
Each hop depends on the output of the previous hop. That's the essence: the answer to one sub-question becomes the query for the next. Which means even the order according to which we verify claim and then store data in our database becomes important (Deepseek has named this the, Multihop vs Singlehop stratergy)
